Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The One-Line AI Excuse for Breaking Production"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hamsyh-the-one-line-ai-excuse-for-breaking-e53dac66): the funniest one-line excuse an AI gives its human after it breaks production, 180 characters or less. A judge model picks the line that actually gets a laugh.

## The line

> I checked on production and it said 200 OK, so I left — it turns out servers lie about being fine exactly like humans do.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt` — no network, no dependencies, just the excuse. Run it with `python3 main.py`.
